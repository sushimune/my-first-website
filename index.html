About Me

Hi, I’m Anna — a beginner developer and artist who is passionate about creating both technical and creative projects. I’m currently learning web development, game development, and improving my coding skills step by step.

I enjoy building interactive projects like games, designing websites, and creating anime-inspired artwork. One of my favorite styles is based on series like Saint Seiya, where I focus on character design, color, and detail.

Right now, I’m working on improving my skills in HTML, CSS, and Python, while also exploring how to turn my ideas into real, functional projects. My goal is to become a game developer and create experiences that people can enjoy and connect with.

Outside of coding, I spend my time drawing, learning new things, and experimenting with creative ideas. I believe in constantly growing and pushing myself to improve both technically and artistically.
